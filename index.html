About Thet Htet Myat Noe

I am Thet Htet Myat Noe, a Graphic Designer and Digital Artist dedicated to transforming strategic business objectives into memorable brand identities, shelf-ready commercial packaging, and evocative digital art.

My portfolio spans 30+ commercial and artistic works — ranging from Myanmar highland Arabica coffee pouches (Oun Pha, Taung Zalat) and regional agricultural packaging (Taunggyi Seeds) to luxury hospitality stationery (Nyein Chan Hotel), esports tournament HUDs (Galaxy Born), and atmospheric digital portraiture.
